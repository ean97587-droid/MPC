Mission

Preparation with purpose.

Our mission is to give McGill students a focused environment to learn about investment banking, private equity and investing while building the judgment, technical fluency and professional habits required to compete for demanding opportunities.

We combine peer mentorship, industry conversations and practical recruiting preparation with a culture that values generosity, trust and sustained effort.

History

A platform built over time.

2017

Founded at McGill University

MPC was established to connect students interested in financial services and capital markets careers.

Today

200 members and a growing network

The organization now supports a broad community of students through recruiting preparation, events, mentorship and industry exposure.

Next

Deepening private-capital exposure

MPC continues to build programming around investing, private equity and the pathways that connect McGill students to leading finance organizations.
