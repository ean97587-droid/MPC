Why MPC

A better environment to get better.

Junior Analysts learn in a setting where finance is treated as a craft: prepare, ask good questions, build the model, understand the business and communicate the answer clearly.

The role also creates access to a peer network that takes recruiting seriously while still making room for mentorship, feedback and shared progress.

The role

What Junior Analysts do

01

Build technical skill

Work through accounting, valuation, transactions, markets and investment concepts in practical contexts.

02

Research and discuss

Study companies, deals and themes, then pressure-test the thinking with other members.

03

Contribute to the community

Help with events, recruiting preparation and the culture that makes the organization useful to its members.

Who should apply

Curious, prepared and dependable.

There is no single prescribed background. Strong candidates show genuine interest in finance, evidence that they can learn quickly, thoughtful communication and the willingness to follow through on commitments.

MPC is especially interested in students who want to become better investors and teammates—not simply add another line to a résumé.

Recruiting process

What to expect

Application

Submit your profile, résumé and required short responses.

Review

The team evaluates preparation, motivation and evidence of fit with the organization.

Interview

Selected candidates discuss their background, interest in finance and how they think through problems.

Decision

Successful candidates join the incoming Junior Analyst cohort and begin onboarding.

Application components

Keep it concise. Make it specific.

Résumé

One-page business or finance résumé in PDF format.

Motivation

Why MPC fits your academic and career goals. 250 words maximum.

Market insight

A private-equity deal, market trend or financial development you found compelling. 250 words maximum.

Leadership

A challenging team dynamic or complex project and the actions you took. 200 words maximum.
